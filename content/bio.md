I am a Master's student in Intelligent Science and Technology at the [University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)](https://www.ustc.edu.cn/), where I am a member of the [Lab for Data Science (LDS)](https://data-science.ustc.edu.cn/). I am fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Xiangnan He](https://hexiangnan.github.io/) and [Prof. Fuli Feng](https://fulifeng.github.io/). I received my B.Eng. in Electronic Information Engineering from USTC in 2025.

My research focuses on making large language models more reliable and effective for reasoning and recommendation. I am particularly interested in reinforcement learning and preference optimization, uncertainty and calibration, and LLM-based recommender and multi-agent systems.

Outside research, I enjoy traveling, discovering local food, and listening to Mandopop.
